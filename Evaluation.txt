Evaluation Approach
The system follows a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) approach, where AI-generated hazards and safety requirements are reviewed, edited, accepted, or rejected by the safety engineer. Since hazard identification depends on the system context and expert judgment, and a validated ground-truth dataset is not available for quantitative comparison, formal accuracy-based evaluation has not been performed. Instead, the system focuses on evidence-based generation, source-document relevance, deterministic ASIL mapping, and engineer review to support the reliability of the results.
